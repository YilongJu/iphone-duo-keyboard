pinyin-dictionary.txt holds the most frequent words of the Pinyin lexicon with their Pinyin and
a frequency. scripts/pinyin-dictionary/build_dictionary.py makes the file from two sources:

  - The dictionary and the language model of the fcitx5 libime project, the files that
    lexicon-NOTICE.txt names for lexicon-pinyin.bin: the words, their Pinyin and the frequency of
    a word without a Wanxiang weight. Both files are under the GNU Lesser General Public
    License, version 2.1 or later (https://www.gnu.org/licenses/old-licenses/lgpl-2.1.html).
  - The word lists of the Wanxiang project (https://github.com/amzxyz/rime-wanxiang, commit
    e52c2958464d23fe2675f7c6770d921926b5d245): the weight of a word, which the file holds as
    its frequency, and the words that scripts/pinyin-dictionary/modern_words.py adds. The lists
    are under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license
    (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

pinyin-dictionary.txt is under the GNU Lesser General Public License, version 2.1 or later, with
the attribution to the Wanxiang project that its license asks for. Changes: the file keeps the
48,000 most frequent words of two or more characters and the characters in common use, leaves
out a reading with less than 5% of a word's uses, and writes the Pinyin without tone marks.
