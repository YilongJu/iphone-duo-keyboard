lexicon-english.bin and lexicon-pinyin.bin hold the word lists and the word-pair and word-triple statistics of the typing
engine in Engine/duo_engine.c. Both files are built from data of other projects.

lexicon-pinyin.bin

  Built by scripts/typing-engine/build_pinyin_lexicon_from_libime.py, in the GitHub workflow
  .github/workflows/pinyin-lexicon.yml, from two files of the fcitx5 libime project:

    https://download.fcitx-im.org/data/lm_sc.arpa-20260629.tar.zst   (language model)
    https://download.fcitx-im.org/data/dict-20260907.tar.zst         (words with their Pinyin)

  Both source files are under the GNU Lesser General Public License, version 2.1 or later
  (https://www.gnu.org/licenses/old-licenses/lgpl-2.1.html), and lexicon-pinyin.bin is under the
  same license. Changes: the converter keeps the single words with their weights and reading
  costs, the 1,250,000 word pairs with the highest weight, the 231,023 further pairs that a kept
  triple needs, all 2,189,434 word triples, and adds the spellings "lve", "nve", "lue" and "nue".
  It stores the pair and triple values in steps of 1/2048 and 1/256 of a natural-log unit. The
  converter and the workflow in this repository rebuild the file from the two source files.

lexicon-english.bin

  Built by scripts/typing-engine/build_english_lexicon.py from three sources:

  - SymSpell's frequency_dictionary_en_82_765.txt and frequency_bigramdictionary_en_243_342.txt,
    MIT license, https://github.com/wolfgarbe/SymSpell
  - Word frequencies of the wordfreq Python package, https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq. Its code
    is under the Apache License 2.0 and its data under CC BY-SA 4.0.
  - Wikipedia's machine-readable list of common misspellings, CC BY-SA 4.0,
    https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Lists_of_common_misspellings/For_machines
    The build removes these misspellings from the word list.
